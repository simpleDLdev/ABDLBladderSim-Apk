# Conditioning v56 — event deck and “I need to pee”

Conditioning sessions no longer roll `MICRO_STD_D6` or `FULL_D20`. A full session and its micros both draw from the conditioning deck: the original 13 events plus habit events picked from the active profile.

The deck follows the quiz. Goals, methods, urgency style, and dimension wants (warning, dribbles, notice, hold) change the weights. Let-go goals drop start-stop guides (a relax step followed by a clench). A surprise mix is mostly short dribbles and spurts, with a full let-go only now and then. More-control goals weight hold drills and potty passes higher. A low tank estimate cannot roll a full void. The same event does not repeat immediately, and the same family does not land three times in a row.

Habit events are things that can happen while you are playing or going about the day: a dribble on a sip, standing up, sitting down, laughing, or concentrating; a “don’t stop it” relax; an autopilot check; a reward after a streak; a slightly longer release once a streak is going. Night goals still have stay-in-bed, sleep-through, and deeper-sleep steps. Direction B keeps scheduled voids, surfing, and wake-to-toilet. A real need still means stop and go. Nothing here asks you to push or to hold until you can’t.

**I need to pee** stays on the session screen for every session type. The tap rolls a pass or an event. Lower control, and a “strict” pass answer, mean fewer passes. More control means more passes and more hold drills. The result goes through the single-item queue, so an open guide is not replaced. The line is written to the log. In conditioning it counts as an urge and can pull the next event forward the same way the urgency slider does.

Sip batches stay at 4 sips and a 30 minute gap unless the profile says otherwise. Safety switches stay optional settings.
