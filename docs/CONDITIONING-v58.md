# Conditioning v58

Pressing **I need to pee** always paints something on screen: a potty-pass banner, an event guide, or “Not enough in the tank yet — potty pass, or try a sip.” The banner is fixed under the status bar on a phone, so it stays in view after you scroll. If a guide is already open, the tap waits in the queue and does not replace it. Every tap is logged.

Urge, when My Conditioning is selected, draws the practice event from the same deck (quiz goals, direction, track, anti-repeat, tank). A low tank says so instead of starting a full void.

Quiz answers map onto the deck tags. `signal` trains fade, `capacity` and `drynight` train more control, and the finer tags (first urge, dribbles, seep, giggle, habit, reward, streak, and the rest) come from the urgency style, the dimension wants, and the sleep answers. The quiz result shows the eligible mix and the About paragraph before you save.

The About paragraph is the level text from `BSControl.describe`, whose percentages are the same chain the Urge roll uses, plus the conditioning counts, sip batch, gap, and I-need-to-pee pass chance.

## 🍼 Infant

You are an infant, baby — totally diaper dependent. You can't really tell when you go; it just happens, and your diaper is there to catch it. Potty trips are almost never on the cards, and changes happen on the babysitter's schedule, once your diaper is good and used. Warning time is tiny — about 1–4 minutes when it comes at all, and waits are hidden. When you press "I need to go", 36% of first answers ask you to wait or hold, and 4% say go straight away. Over a whole potty attempt you make it to the potty 10% of the time; 9% end mostly dry with just a small dribble, 32% are a small accident and 49% a major one. Change time usually comes once you report your gear at about 75% saturation ("May I change?" says yes 20% of the time past that point). Recommended gear: thick diapers in the day, thick diapers at night.
