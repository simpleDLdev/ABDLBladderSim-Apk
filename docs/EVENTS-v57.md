Let-go goals are mostly micros (about 65–85%), with full let-go macros mixed in. Bedwetting leans the other way: more full releases at night. Direction B is the more-control set (holds, surfing, gentle kegels, scheduled voids, waking).

A real need still means stop and go. Guides for let-go events do not clench after a release. Safety switches stay optional settings.
