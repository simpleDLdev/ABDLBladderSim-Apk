# Conditioning v60 — a natural night

Night conditioning no longer runs the session event timer. After the bedtime routine it behaves like Natural Night: a silent night, then a morning check-in.

There is no countdown, no "next event in", no prerolled or timed event, no sip prompt, no scheduled alarm, and no tank ETA. Day conditioning is unchanged. The single-item queue still holds a second tap while a guide is on screen.

## What can still happen overnight

- **I woke up** asks whether you have an urge, are already wet, or are dry. Each answer draws that night-deck event.
- **I need to pee** draws from the night deck. It does not start a timer or pull the next event forward.
- **DryClip**, only if you opted in during the routine. Quiet logs the wet time. Gentle is a soft stay-asleep or get-up line. Alarm wakes you fully. If you leave it off, nothing from the clip fires.
- **Morning check-in** at the wake time you set. It logs wakes, wets, when, saturation, feeling, and any clip times. If DryClip was on, its morning check-in opens too.

## Notifications

While a night is in progress the app schedules only:

1. The morning check-in at the routine's wake time.
2. DryClip, and only when you opted in.

Opening the app again, or restarting it, does not put the event timer back. A bedtime routine that is still unfinished is also timer-free.

## Day

A day session still prerolls the next event, shows the countdown and the tank ETA, and schedules the usual main, micro, and sip notifications.
