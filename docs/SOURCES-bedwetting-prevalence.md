# Sources — bedwetting fun facts (My Potty Profile, v8)

Used for the "Fun facts" section of `js/potty-report.js`. Figures are prevalence estimates; definitions
and populations vary between studies, so ranges are quoted. Game/roleplay app — not medical advice.

| Fact in app | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 5-year-olds who wet the bed | ~15–20% | Am Fam Physician 2022;106(5) "Enuresis in Children: Common Questions and Answers" — https://www.aafp.org/afp/2022/1100/enuresis-children ; ~15% in "Approach to nocturnal enuresis in children" (2024) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11132618/ |
| 7-year-olds | 5–10% | EAU Guidelines on Paediatric Urology, Monosymptomatic Nocturnal Enuresis — https://uroweb.org/guidelines/paediatric-urology/chapter/monosymptomatic-nocturnal-enuresis-bedwetting |
| 8-year-olds / 12-year-olds | 7–10% / 2–3% | "Management of nocturnal enuresis – myths and facts" (World J Nephrol 2016) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4936340/ |
| Late teens | ~1–2% (by 17 y) | AAFP 2022; EAU (1–2% adolescents) |
| Adults | ~0.5–1% (some data 2–3%) | AAFP 2022; PMC11132618 (0.5–1%); PMC4936340 (2–3% if untreated) |
| Spontaneous resolution | ~14–15% per year | AAFP 2022 (14%); EAU (15%/yr at any age); PMC4936340 (14–16%) |
| Family history | 15% none / 44% one parent / 77% both | Bakwin 1973, cited by AAFP 2022, EAU, PMC4936340 |
| Sex ratio | ~2 boys : 1 girl | EAU; PMC11132618 |
| Organic cause | <3% of clinic referrals | PMC11132618 |
| Urination time "law" (calibration) | 21 ± 13 s | Yang et al., PNAS 2014 (see docs/CALIBRATION-QUIZ-CAMPAIGN.md) |
