# Conditioning v59 — night

Day conditioning draws only day events. Night conditioning draws only night events. After the bedtime routine the session goes quiet: no sip prompts, no day events, no alarms, unless you opted into DryClip.

**I woke up** asks whether you have an urge, are already wet, or are dry, then draws that night subset. Less control stays in bed. More control gets up. Morning check-in logs wakes, wets, when, saturation, feeling, and any clip times.

## Less control (Infant night)

1. **Evening drinks** (skippable)

A bedtime drink is fine if you are thirsty. One small one. It adds a little to the night tank.

2. **Last potty, or skip it** (skippable)

You can skip the last potty on purpose. Staying in bed is the practice.

3. **What to wear to bed**

Wear a thick diaper to bed. It holds a full night, so a change can wait until morning.

4. **Gear check-in** (skippable)

Confirm what you actually put on. The morning log compares against this.

5. **Wind down** (skippable)

Same bedtime, dark room, one short phrase, then stop deciding things. Deeper sleep is the goal.

6. **Affirmation** (skippable)

If you wake, you can stay in bed and let go. A real need still means get up.

7. **Wake time** (skippable)

Set the time morning starts. Until then there are no sip prompts and no day events.

8. **DryClip (opt in)** (skippable)

DryClip sleep mode is off until you turn it on. Suggested if you do: quiet, from your night-alarm answer. Quiet logs the wet time with no alert. Gentle is a soft stay-asleep or get-up line. Alarm wakes you fully.

DryClip is off until you opt in. If you turn it on, this profile suggests **quiet**.

## More control (Little Kid night)

1. **Evening drinks** (skippable)

Cut off drinks well before bed. The last sip was earlier on purpose.

2. **Last potty** (skippable)

Empty before lights out. That is the bedtime void, not a test of how long you can wait.

3. **What to wear to bed**

Wear a pad to bed. A backup for a mostly dry night.

4. **Gear check-in** (skippable)

Confirm what you actually put on. The morning log compares against this.

5. **Wind down** (skippable)

Lights down when you are ready. After this the session stays quiet.

6. **Affirmation** (skippable)

If you wake with an urge, you get up and finish in the toilet. A real need still means go.

7. **Wake time** (skippable)

Set the time morning starts. Until then there are no sip prompts and no day events.

8. **DryClip (opt in)** (skippable)

DryClip sleep mode is off until you turn it on. Suggested if you do: alarm, from your night-alarm answer. Quiet logs the wet time with no alert. Gentle is a soft stay-asleep or get-up line. Alarm wakes you fully.

DryClip is off until you opt in. If you turn it on, this profile suggests **alarm**.
